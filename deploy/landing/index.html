Privacy first

Your Conversations. Your Space.

Stay connected without unnecessary tracking or advertising. Emix is designed to put your conversations first, not turn your activity into a product.

  • No ads. No analytics. Emix shows no advertising and collects no usage statistics or tracking data.
  • Encrypted in transit. Messages, calls and screen shares travel over encrypted connections (TLS, and DTLS-SRTP for calls).
  • Invite-only. New accounts need an invitation, so your community stays the people you chose.

High-quality video

See Every Detail.

Experience crisp, high-quality video calls with advanced resolution settings and support for up to 4K on compatible devices and connections.

  • You choose the quality. 720p, 1080p, 1440p or 4K, at 30 or 60 fps, limited only by your camera, computer and connection.
  • Smooth on any connection. Each viewer automatically receives the quality their screen and network can handle.
  • Hardware accelerated. Emix uses your graphics card for video encoding where it is supported.

Screen sharing

Share Without Compromise.

From presentations to gaming, share your screen with clarity and control. Choose the quality settings that work best for your setup.

  • Presets for what you share. Sharp text for slides and code, smooth motion for games, or maximum quality.
  • Up to 4K at 60 fps. Or your screen's native resolution, when your computer and connection allow it.
  • Cameras and screens together. Keep your camera on while you share, and see everyone else while you watch.
