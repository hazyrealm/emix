Built for great calls with friends

Emix is tuned for small calls, where two people can both run their cameras and share their screens at the same time.

  • Camera quality you choose. 720p, 1080p, 1440p or 4K at 30 or 60 fps, limited only by your camera, computer and connection.
  • Screen sharing presets. Text and slides stay sharp; games stay smooth; high quality sends full resolution when bandwidth allows.
  • Adapts to the connection. When a network or device can't keep up, viewers automatically get a lighter stream instead of a frozen one.
  • Uses your graphics card. Video is encoded with hardware acceleration where your GPU supports it, and Emix shows you whether it is.
